# Amendment 01 to the Milestone Facilities Grant Program Agreement

**Amends:** Milestone Facilities Grant — Program Agreement, effective 1 July 2026
**Effective:** 20 August 2026
**Authority:** Regional Development Office, Finance Committee resolution 2026/14

---

## 1. Preamble

1.1 Following the Finance Committee's review of disbursement controls, the
Agreement is amended as set out below. All other provisions remain in force.

## 2. Authorisation threshold

2.1 Clause 4.2 is amended such that the threshold shall read twenty-five thousand
dollars ($25,000). Any disbursement in excess of twenty-five thousand dollars
($25,000) shall require the counter-signature of the Chief Financial Officer.

2.2 The amended threshold applies to any disbursement not yet released as at the
effective date of this Amendment, including disbursements against Awards approved
before that date.

## 3. Disbursement timeliness

3.1 Clause 5.4 is amended such that funds shall be released to the provider within
seven (7) days of the tranche being authorised. The escalation point in that clause
is correspondingly amended to five days.

3.2 The amended period applies to tranches already in flight as at the effective
date.

## 4. Evidence

4.1 Clause 4.5 continues to apply without amendment.

4.2 The administering body may specify the form in which evidence is to be
furnished.

4.3 Evidence found to be materially incomplete shall be returned to the provider
with reasons.

4.4 Evidence of completion shall be submitted promptly upon certification.

## 5. Provider acknowledgement

5.1 Providers shall be notified of this Amendment within ten days of its effective
date.

5.2 A provider shall acknowledge this Amendment before any further tranche is
released against their Award. The acknowledgement shall be recorded against the
Award.

## 6. Interpretation

6.1 Where this Amendment conflicts with the Agreement, this Amendment prevails.
